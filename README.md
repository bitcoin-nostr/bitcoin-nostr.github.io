## 📖 Sample Lessons

### Bitcoin B1.1: "What is Money?"
Discover the fundamental properties that make something "money" - durability, divisibility, portability, and scarcity. Explore how different civilizations throughout history have used shells, gold, silver, and other forms of money. Learn why these properties matter and how Bitcoin perfects them in the digital age.
*~5 minutes + interactive quiz*

### Bitcoin B2.3: "Your First Bitcoin Transaction"  
Step-by-step walkthrough of what happens when you send Bitcoin. Follow a real transaction from your wallet, through the network, to the recipient. Understand mining, confirmations, and fees in simple terms without overwhelming technical details.
*~5 minutes + hands-on simulation*

### Nostr N1.2: "Creating Your Nostr Identity"
Learn how to generate your first Nostr key pair and understand the relationship between your public and private keys. Discover how this cryptographic identity gives you true ownership of your digital presence, unlike traditional social media accounts.
*~5 minutes + practical setup*

### Nostr N3.4: "Lightning Zaps Explained"
Understand how Lightning Network integration enables instant, low-cost payments on Nostr. Learn to send your first "zap" (Bitcoin tip) to content creators and discover how this creates new economic models for content and social interaction.
*~5 minutes + live demonstration*
